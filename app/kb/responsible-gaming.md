# Responsible gaming

You can set daily, weekly or monthly deposit limits in Settings → Responsible gaming. A lower limit applies immediately; a higher limit applies after 24 hours.

Self-exclusion blocks your account for 6 months, 1 year or permanently. It cannot be cancelled before the end of the chosen period.

A cooling-off period pauses your account for 24 hours to 6 weeks. During the pause you cannot deposit or place bets.
