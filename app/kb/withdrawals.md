# Withdrawals

Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours after the request is approved. Bank cards usually receive funds in 1-3 business days, e-wallets within a few hours.

Before the first withdrawal the account must pass identity verification (KYC): upload a photo of an ID document and a proof of address in the Verification section of your profile.

A withdrawal is always sent to the same payment method that was used for the deposit. If that method is no longer available, contact support to choose another one.
